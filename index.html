Technology-Driven Pharmaceutical Distribution

1. Company Overview

DrugStoc is a technology-driven pharmaceutical distribution company focused on improving access to genuine medicines and strengthening the pharmaceutical supply chain in Africa.

It's platform provides healthcare providers with access to medicines, real-time stock visibility, ordering and delivery services.

2. Business Problem

Healthcare providers may struggle to maintain optimal medicine inventory while avoiding stockouts, overstocking and inefficient procurement.

To provide real-time stock visibility and ordering, a suitable business problem to analyse is:

How can DrugStoc improve inventory forecasting and proactively alert pharmacies when they are likely to run out of important medicines?

This could help pharmacies reduce stockouts while also reducing unnecessary excess inventory.

3. Stakeholders

  1. Pharmacies
  2. Hospital pharmacies
  3. Pharmacists
  4. DrugStoc procurement team
  5. DrugStoc warehouse/inventory team
  6. Suppliers/manufacturers
  7. Logistics/delivery teams
  8. Customers/patients
  9. Compliance/regulatory teams

4. Business Need

To improve medicine availability and inventory efficiency by using historical ordering data, current stock levels and purchasing patterns to identify potential stockouts before they occur.

5a. Functional Requirements

FR1 — Inventory Monitoring

The system should continuously monitor stock levels for medicines purchased by each pharmacy.

FR2 — Stockout Alerts

The system should automatically notify a pharmacy when projected inventory falls below its defined reorder threshold.

FR3 — Demand Forecasting

The system should use historical purchasing data to estimate future medicine demand and recommend appropriate reorder quantities.

5b. Business Requirements

BR1 — Reduce Stockouts

Reduce the frequency and duration of medicine stockouts among participating pharmacies.

BR2 — Improve Inventory Efficiency

Reduce excess inventory and associated holding costs.

BR3 — Improve Customer Service

Increase the availability of commonly requested medicines and improve pharmacy satisfaction.

6. Recommended Solution

Develop an AI/Data-Driven Inventory Forecasting and Replenishment System using the following structure:

Data → Demand Forecast → Stockout Risk → Alert → Recommended Order → Delivery

The solution should also include a dashboard to monitor important supply-chain and inventory KPIs:

  • Stockout Rate
  • Inventory Turnover
  • Forecast Accuracy
  • Average Days of Inventory
  • Order Fulfilment Rate
  • On-Time Delivery
  • Cancelled Orders

Supply-Chain and Inventory KPIs

The following table provides definitions, formulas and simple examples for the key supply-chain and inventory KPIs.

KPI Definition Formula Simple Example
1. Stockout Rate Percentage of times/items where required stock was unavailable when needed. (Stockout events ÷ Total demand/ordering opportunities) × 100 20 ÷ 500 × 100 = 4%
2. Inventory Turnover Measures how many times inventory is sold/used and replenished during a period. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) ÷ Average Inventory £500,000 ÷ £100,000 = 5 times
3. Forecast Accuracy Measures how close forecasted demand is to actual demand. [1 − |Actual − Forecast| ÷ Actual] × 100 Actual = 1,000
Forecast = 900

4. Average Days of Inventory Estimates how many days the current inventory will cover based on consumption or sales. Average Inventory ÷ COGS × Number of Days £100k ÷ £500k × 365 = 73 days
5. Order Fulfilment Rate Percentage of customer orders successfully fulfilled, usually completely and correctly. (Orders fulfilled ÷ Total orders) × 100 950 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 95%
6. On-Time Delivery Percentage of orders delivered by the promised delivery date or time. (Orders delivered on time ÷ Total delivered orders) × 100 920 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 92%
7. Cancelled Orders Number or percentage of orders cancelled before fulfilment. Cancellation Rate = (Cancelled orders ÷ Total orders) × 100 30 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 3%
